# HQ narrative revision - exile, bargain and dream departure

## Approval and scope

The user approved one plan to record this narrative revision and to generate exactly the three visual reference boards H-01, H-02 and H-03 in `.atena/generated/2026-10-03-hq-narrative-revision/`. This is a reference-only approval; it does not authorize runtime admission, external dispatch, publication, or deletion.

## H-01 - opening comic

- The Golden City is crowded and inhabited only by elves.
- Its council directs the principal families to leave separately, each seeking a new home for the elves.
- The Thalestriel caravan leaves with more than one hundred elves, family relics, and its open wagon.
- On the journey, creatures, hostile enemies and natural disasters repeatedly strike the caravan. Whenever Lolth appears, she is visibly cursing and blaming Eol.
- The plague then kills the great majority of the Thalestriel. Lolth is unaffected; she and exactly eight survivors reach a cave with the damaged wagon.

## H-02 - after the first boss

- Shar appears because she wants to meet the mortal who survives every calamity Eol directs at the Thalestriel while Lolth remains untouched.
- Lolth offers her memories of having been a goddess in the previous plane in exchange for a way to gain power and become divine in this plane.
- Shar accepts, uses the wand called O Beijo de Shar to place the Shadow Mark on Lolth, transforms her into a drow, and gives Lolth the wand.
- The wand-holder can command every shadow creature.

## H-03 - finale

- On completing her transformation, Lolth opens a portal to the Dream Plane.
- Lolth and the eight drows enter together. In the Golden City of Dreams, Lolth leaves O Beijo de Shar in their custody.

## Reconciliation

This supersedes the short C-01 prologue board for narrative continuity only; C-01 remains retained as a historical generated candidate. The existing core remains: Lolth is initially immune to the plague, exactly eight Thalestriel survive, the first Mark follows the first boss, and the journey ends with all nine together in the Dream Plane. The accepted exchange is specifically Lolth's former-divinity memories; no additional loss of memory by the eight drows is implied by this revision.
