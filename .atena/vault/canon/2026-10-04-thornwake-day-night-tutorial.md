# Thornwake day/night tutorial

## Day salvage and repair

The B-02 tutorial begins at the day-start Thornwake cave camp. Lolth gathers physical salvage, returns it to the wagon, transfers it to the protected five-slot wagon stock, and crafts a single explicit Wheel Kit recipe. Completing the Wheel Kit repairs the wagon and changes its state from `cave_damaged` to `stationed`. This repair never unlocks travel: the wagon remains `travel_locked` until Mark IV, four cures, and an eligible puller.

## First night defense

After the repair tutorial, night begins through a clear transition. Only Briar Hounds and Stags of Mire threaten the cave camp and wagon. Their attacks are telegraphed. A successful defense returns the player to a safe camp state. Before Mark I, Lolth's death, wagon destruction, or a plagued-ally failure restores the B-01 cave-start state with no permanent loss.

## Progression boundary

This tutorial awards no Shadow Echoes, Marks, cure access, or travel. Antlered Hunger, Shar, THE KISS OF SHAR, Mark I, FIRST THREAD, and the first cure are B-03 work. The numeric recipe, duration, wave, health, and damage values are implementation data subject to playtesting; they are not canon.

## Presentation and boundaries

All new player-facing text, code comments, implementation notes, and reports are English. Current reference boards remain outside `res://`; no art is admitted, regenerated, or shipped by B-02. B-02 does not implement later regions, Web Anchors, posts, pullers, the wagon's travel state, or a runtime rebuild.
