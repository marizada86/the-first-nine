# Nolf Xiar and Xiar's kiss

The protagonist is now **Nolf**, the deity previously named Shar is now **Xiar**, and the wand is **Xiar's kiss**. This owner-approved naming decision applies throughout the current game presentation, narrative and future production.

| Previous name | Current name |
| --- | --- |
| Lolth | Nolf |
| Shar | Xiar |
| Kiss of Shar, The Kiss of Shar, O Beijo de Shar | Xiar's kiss |

All other names and established character traits, story events, visuals, gameplay and scope remain as they are. This explicitly includes Drow/Drows, Eol, Thalestriel, the eight survivor names, regions and The First Nine.

Historical approvals, evidence, audits and generation receipts preserve their original wording. Older names in those records refer to the same characters and wand. Existing filenames, paths, resource IDs and internal identifiers remain compatibility aliases; they are not the displayed names. No asset regeneration or filename migration is part of this decision.

This supersedes the nomenclature in earlier canonical decisions, without retroactively changing their approval history. Current canonical prose and the B-09 panel script are reconciled with this amendment. It does not expand art production, admit comic art into the game, authorize publication or certify submission eligibility.
