# First boss, Mark I and first cure

## Boss hand-off

After the B-02 safe camp, the player deliberately begins the Antlered Hunger encounter through one English camp action. The boss is the only B-03 combat encounter and uses readable attacks and telegraphs. Its defeat is the sole trigger for the post-boss hand-off.

## Shar shell and Mark I

Boss defeat enters a neutral, skippable Shar shell. It uses no H-02 board, old comic line, unapproved final dialogue, or player-facing text beyond approved English controls. The shell ends by applying Mark I.

Mark I transforms Lolth from elf to drow and adds `FIRST THREAD`, a short-range shadow strike that supplements, never replaces, her existing melee attack and dodge.

## First cure and Echo boundary

The cure interface presents all eight plagued Thalestriel by name and requires exactly one selection. The chosen person becomes a non-controllable drow. The other seven remain plagued. The wagon remains `stationed` and `travel_locked`.

Shadow Echo collection begins only after this cure. Echoes are capped at the next Mark threshold: Mark II, a second cure, travel, and every later effect remain unavailable until later approved work.

## Boundaries

All new player-facing text, comments, documentation, and reports are English. This decision admits no art, does not change the H-02 script or its references, and does not authorize Stonehook, wagon travel, pullers, posts, Web Anchors, later regions, a push, pull request, merge, or publication.
