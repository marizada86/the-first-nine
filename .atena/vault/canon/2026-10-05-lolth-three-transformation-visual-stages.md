# Lolth: visual stages at Marks III, V and VII

On 2026-10-05, after delivery, the owner replied `aprovados`, accepting the three produced skins and animations at Marks III, V and VII. This curation receipt closes the human review recorded in [[2026-10-05-lolth-three-transformations-production]].

The owner approved three additional visual transformations and their bounded local presentation integration. Mark 0 remains elf and Marks I–II remain base drow. Mark III adds compact shadow armour and a pulse transformation; Mark V adds four folded spectral spider appendages and echo presentation; Mark VII adds eight spectral spider appendages and web presentation.

Stages persist through the following threshold. The Mark IX prototype may retain stage VII as a clearly documented placeholder, not an implementation of the final SHADOW CROWN spider form. The humanoid body, white hair, identity, baseline and opaque trousers through the boots persist in all three stages.

The appendages are shadow manifestations, not wings or a flight power. This decision adds no powers, new attacks, movement buffs, collision changes, cure changes or revision of canonical ability order. Existing minimalist and full-trousers rules remain binding. New assets are admitted only after the technical and visual checks in the approved spec pass. External publication and sharing are not authorized.
