# B-00 resolution - opening, ending and production boundary

Naming amended on 2026-10-05 by the owner's explicit instruction: Nolf, Xiar and Xiar's kiss. Historical record IDs and asset paths retain their original names.


## Opening and travel

The cave is in Thornwake Forest. Antlered Hunger is the first boss. The wagon remains at the cave camp after Mark I and is `travel_locked` until Mark IV, when four Thalestriel have been cured and an eligible puller is assigned. Nolf explores the early Thornwake and Stonehook-adjacent routes on foot; a first cure does not authorize wagon travel to Stonehook.

The previous statement that the wagon may travel to Stonehook immediately after the first cure is superseded.

## Tutorial threats and Mark I

The first night defense uses Thornwake's canonical Briar Hounds and Stags of Mire, never a generic species called “shadow creatures.” Xiar's kiss can command shadow creatures in lore, but introduces no player command mechanic in the vertical slice.

Nolf starts with a direct melee attack and a short dodge. Mark I, `FIRST THREAD`, adds a short-range shadow strike; it supplements rather than replaces the base combat kit.

## Failure, saves and allies

Before Mark I, death of Nolf, wagon destruction, or the failure state of a plagued ally restores the cave-camp start state after the skippable H-01 comic. No Thalestriel is permanently lost. After Mark I, a safe return to the wagon saves operational progress; a failure restores the last safe wagon state. Mark ownership remains narrative and cure progression, not the operational save system.

## Web Anchors and route geometry

`BLACK PULSE` remains Mark III's combat ability. Its Web 2 proficiency also permits the first permanent Web Anchor; this does not replace `BLACK PULSE`. `SPIDER'S PROMISE` at Mark VII remains the advanced anchor power for extended or more demanding crossings. Every anchor creates a fixed, flat, continuous wagon base. Optional platforms are permitted for Nolf-only side routes and are forbidden on the main wagon route.

## Ending resolution

H-03 is authoritative for the ending. Nolf alone trades memories of her former divinity; the eight drows retain their memories. Nolf, all eight drows, the open wagon and all family relics cross the Dream Plane portal together. Nolf remains with the group in the Golden City of Dreams and leaves the wand there in the eight drows' collective custody.

The sole English player-facing name of the wand is `THE KISS OF SHAR`. It is never displayed as “Xiar's kiss” in player-facing text.

## Production boundary

The jam delivery target is a polished vertical slice from H-01 through the first cure. Existing later-region runtime work remains prototype material until it is reconciled against this document. Do not rebuild the runtime before the deadline: extend it only in small, tested batches and record the scene-based reconstruction as post-jam technical debt.

The current generated boards remain reference-only. H-02 must not ship unchanged because it contains explicit nudity; H-03 must not ship unchanged because it depicts a covered wagon. Any runtime comic conversion or image regeneration requires a separate approved art-admission plan. All new player-facing text, documentation and implementation output is in English.
