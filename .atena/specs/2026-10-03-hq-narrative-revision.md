# Plan - narrative HQ revision

## Acceptance criteria

- H-01 shows the elf-only Golden City, council, more-than-one-hundred-elf exodus, Eol's calamities, plague loss and cave arrival with Lolth plus eight survivors.
- H-02 shows Shar's reason for appearing, Lolth's former-goddess-memory bargain, the Shadow Mark, drow transformation and wand transfer.
- H-03 shows the completed transformation, portal, the united group entering the Dream Plane, and the wand left with the drows in the Golden City of Dreams.
- Every H-01 appearance of Lolth visibly conveys that she is cursing and blaming Eol; final player-facing dialogue remains an English-writing task, not AI-rendered text.
- No candidate has readable AI text, watermark, logo, a horse, a second wagon, or non-elf residents in the initial Golden City.
