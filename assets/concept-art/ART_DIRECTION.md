# The First Nine — Art Direction v1

## North star

An original, painterly dark-fantasy game world where a fragile **warm gold** survives inside **cold blue** ruins and **violet-black** shadow magic. The player must always read the caravan as safety, the shadows as risk, and the Dream Plane as beautiful but unfamiliar.

## Visual rules

- **Gold / ember:** memory, shelter, salvage, the caravan flame, elven craft.
- **Cobalt / blue-black:** Eol's ruined Material Plane, distance, weather, danger.
- **Violet / charcoal:** Shar, forgetting, Shadow Echoes and the Mark.
- **Ivory / pale gold:** the City of Dreams; serene, not triumphant.
- Use strong side-view silhouettes and reserve busy detail for key art and HQ panels, never gameplay collision space.
- Reuse a single abstract **thorn-and-web** emblem. Do not use existing fantasy IP symbols.

## Character direction

`../references/lolth-user-reference.png` and `../references/lolth-anatomy-reference-01.png` through `04.png` are the approved visual references for Lolth. Preserve their defining language without reproducing the artworks: pale blue-gray drow skin, very long white hair, elongated ears, white segmented chitin grown like ritual armor, and asymmetric spider limbs emerging from the silhouette.

Lolth begins as a physically powerful elf; `lolth-original-elf-form-v1.png` is the sole source for this pre-pact form. `FIRST THREAD` (Mark I) makes her the first drow. Each of the eight Mark stages grows the white segmented plates and increases violet-black webbing. At `SHADOW CROWN`, **Lolth's complete drow body is the spider's head/front**: her face, torso, arms, hair and chitin command the cephalothorax. The abdomen and eight legs are a creature of black-violet shadow, smoke and web, not solid flesh. She is never a rider on a spider, a detached humanoid face in a spider head, or a conventional flesh spider. Her palette is **bone-white chitin, silver hair, bruised violet shadow, and small antique-gold relic details** — not a black-cloak-only design.

The eight survivors must use the same family materials but different secondary accents and tools. Their pre-pact and awakened sheets are paired references: preserve their role, gender presentation, facial identity and signature tool across the transformation. Their approved names and production roles are `AELIRA` (Forager), `VAELUN` (Porter), `NIMARA` (Scout), `THAVIEL` (Keeper of the Flame), `ILYREN` (Mender), `ORISYA` (Healer), `SORETH` (Warden), and `LURAEN` (Dreamer). The canonical roster is recorded in `.atena/vault/canon/2026-10-03-thalestriel-survivor-names.md`.
